Information collected

Radio Outdoors stores account identity, email address, profile information, Adventures, Journals, Locations, contacts, photos, and the policy versions you accept. Email is private by default. This acceptance workflow does not collect IP addresses or browser fingerprints.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, provide community features, secure the service, recover accounts, moderate content, and respond to support requests. Radio Outdoors does not need raw payment-card information for these features.

Photo moderation

Uploaded photos remain private until processed under the moderation policy. Automated image moderation may be performed by OpenAI. Automated systems can make mistakes. Authorized staff may review, approve, reject, quarantine, or remove content. Contact {{ radio_outdoors_contact_email }} regarding a moderation decision.

Storage and service providers

Necessary service providers may process limited information for hosting, email, maps, storage, security, backups, and moderation. Radio Outdoors does not publish administrator credentials or private moderation data.

Your choices

Use account privacy controls where available. To request access, correction, or account/data deletion, email {{ radio_outdoors_contact_email }}.
