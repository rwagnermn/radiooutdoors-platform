Using Radio Outdoors

Radio Outdoors is an amateur-radio community for sharing outdoor operating experiences. Use the service lawfully, provide accurate account information, protect your password, and respect other people and their content.

Member content

You retain ownership of content you submit. You give Radio Outdoors permission to store, process, resize, moderate, and display that content as needed to operate the service and honor your visibility choices.

Community safety

Content must follow the Community and Photo Standards. Radio Outdoors may restrict, quarantine, or remove content or accounts when needed for safety, legal compliance, or service integrity.

Service availability

The Alpha service may change and is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability. Outdoor and operating information must not replace official safety guidance.
