What is Radio Outdoors?

Radio Outdoors is your storybook of ham radio adventures. It is not a contest, awards program, social feed, or replacement for a logbook.

Create an Adventure

Choose a title, named Location, and exact Operating Position. Add a Journal entry telling what happened. One sentence is enough.

Operating Positions

The exact place where the station was set up. A Location may have several positions. Blue pins mark Operating Positions.

Map Pin Colors

Blue means Operating Position. Yellow means an Open Adventure. Red means an Operating Advisory should be read before making the trip.

Public and Private

Public Adventures can be viewed by anyone. Private Adventures are visible only to their owner and administrators.

Members and Followers

A Member has a QRZ-verified callsign and can publish Adventures. A Follower can request notifications.

Member Email

A Member can make an email visible to other signed-in Members or keep it private.

Photos

Photos are optional. The story matters even when there are no photographs.
