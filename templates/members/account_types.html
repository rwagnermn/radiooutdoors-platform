Who can use Radio Outdoors?

Joe Public

Anyone can view Public Adventures, Journals, photos, and Member profiles without creating an account.

Follower

Has an invitation-only, callsign-free account that can follow Members and receive notifications. A Follower is not a Radio Outdoors Member and cannot publish content.

Member

A Registered Member with a QRZ-verified amateur-radio callsign. Members can publish Adventures and Journals, upload photos, share stories, and approve or block their Followers.
