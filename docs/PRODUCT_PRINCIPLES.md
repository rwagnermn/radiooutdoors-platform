# Radio Outdoors Product Principles

1. Radio Outdoors is about preserving experiences, not merely contacts.
2. Adventures are the core user story.
3. Keep workflows simple and obvious.
4. Do not reproduce information already readily available elsewhere unless it directly supports the Adventure workflow.
5. Users should not have to leave a workflow merely to create required supporting data.
6. Locations and Operating Positions should help people discover and remember real operating experiences.
7. Keep the application readable and usable outdoors.
8. Prefer KISS over unnecessary categories, settings, and explanation.
9. Preserve member-created content and the project’s long-term continuity.
10. Design for sustainability beyond the original developer or administrator.

These principles guide scope and product decisions. When a proposed feature conflicts with them, document the tradeoff before implementation.
